Low-Rank Steering for Mixture-of-Experts LLMs 2026

A 197K-parameter learned head ranks which experts react to context, and the top ones are switched on or off at inference with no change to the model's weights. Trained on contrastive pairs from SQuAD, HotpotQA and FEVER, it improves mean faithfulness on OLMoE by +0.101 across six benchmarks, about 4× the gain of the published SteerMoE baseline, and transfers across six MoE families from 1.3B to 30.5B parameters. First author with Mahdi Salmani; full paper in preparation.

Adversarial Co-Evolution of RL & LLM Agents 2026

Gin Rummy agents trained with distributed PPO and TRPO, measured against a fixed rule-based expert used only as a yardstick. Across 100+ controlled runs, five training choices lift the agent from about 30% to 34.2 ± 2.1% against the expert, and four that looked promising, including imitating an LLM guide and training against a live LLM opponent, do not help. The paper is accepted at AIIDE 2026 (AAAI).
