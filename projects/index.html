Footnote: RAG for SEC Filings ↗

2026

A question-answering assistant for company filings that names the page behind every answer: page-bounded chunks, hybrid BM25 and vector search with a cross-encoder reranker, routing by company and year, and a LangGraph agent allowed one extra search. On all 150 FinanceBench questions the agent is right 85.8% of the time (87.2% on the 99 held-back questions), against 33.6% with no documents. Amended copies of real statement pages show the risk: with both versions in front of the model, answers lead with the old amount up to 100% of the time, and filing dates plus one sentence that the later filing wins bring it to 0% on three models.

LangChain · LangGraph · Chroma · FastAPI · MCP · Google ADK

Differentiating Through Dual Prices ↗

2026

Decision-focused learning for treatment policies under a capacity limit, trained through Lagrangian dual prices by implicit differentiation. Co-first author, accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 ML×OR workshop; I was the largest code contributor (41 of 73 commits). code

Tri-Modal Sentiment Analysis — Benchmarked & Deployed ↗

2026

Text, facial expression, and voice prosody fused into one model scoring 74.5% accuracy / 0.743 weighted-F1 on MELD (2,607 utterances) — the level of published multimodal systems. A RoBERTa-large + ModernBERT-large ensemble, an MTCNN→FER-ViT face branch at 98.5% coverage, and a frozen Whisper encoder with learnable layer weighting. It started as a 2022 course project stuck at 63% on its original corpus, where newer backbones moved it only half a point, so the limit was the data and I rebuilt it on MELD. Runs live on scale-to-zero Cloud Run under $1/month, with CI on every push and deploys gated by a smoke test that fails on degraded responses. Two browser calibration labs personalize it to a user in five minutes — voice to 96.7%, face to 90.5% leave-one-out. Playground · voice lab · face lab · code.

RoBERTa · ModernBERT · DINOv2 · Whisper · MTCNN · FastAPI · Cloud Run · GitHub Actions

In-Hand Cube Rotation on a Simulated LEAP Hand ↗

2026

PPO policies for HORA-style in-hand rotation in mjlab (MuJoCo Warp), from proprioception only: 16 runs and 145.5 GPU-hours, each policy tested in 7 conditions at 1,024 episodes. Randomizing friction and cube mass in training was the one significant gain on slippery cubes (0.378 vs 0.280 rad/s at friction 0.3), a proportional drift gate cut tilt error 13%, and I found a reset-reference bug in mjlab 1.1.1, then ported the task to mjlab 1.6. Simulation only.

PyTorch · MuJoCo Warp · RSL-RL · SLURM
